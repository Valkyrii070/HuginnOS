# Install Huginn OS
Put Huginn OS on a computer, from the installation drive.

## Before you start
Huginn OS needs about 20 GB. To keep Windows beside it, shut Windows down fully first (turn off Fast Startup in Windows' Power Options), and back up anything important either way.

## The dock
The installer fills the screen. Each step is an island in the dock at the bottom: the one you're on is raised and lit, and done ones keep your answer, so you can click any of them to change it. The orb at the end of the dock shows everything once more; nothing on the computer changes until you press Install there. The top bar shows the network and the power, and the person button opens help seeing, hearing and typing at any step.

## The steps
**Language**: choose it, then **Try it first** (nothing changes; Install is in the dock whenever you like) or **Install Huginn OS**. Help seeing, hearing and typing is right there too, and stays on once Huginn is installed. **Keyboard**: pick the layout on your keys and try it. **Ready?**: Huginn looks the computer over (power, room, the internet, Secure Boot, Windows' Fast Startup and BitLocker) and says what works out of the box; join Wi‑Fi here if you like. **Where it lives**: erase a drive, use free space, make room beside Windows, or choose partitions yourself. **Extras**: media formats (downloaded from Ubuntu, built on this computer, or not now), Microsoft's fonts, fonts for every script, NVIDIA's driver and printer drivers. **Apps**: what comes with Huginn, game-development tools, Flathub, and Linux versions of the apps found in Windows. **From Windows**: your documents, pictures, music, videos, desktop and downloads, copied or used where they are, and your browsers' bookmarks (Edge, Chrome, Firefox and others) into Huginn's Favorites. Saved passwords and Wi‑Fi keys stay in Windows. **You**, **Time zone**, and **Safety**: encryption with a recovery key (and, on a computer with a TPM 2.0 security chip, a short PIN at start-up instead of the password), restore points, the firewall and security fixes.

## Media formats
**Download them from Ubuntu** adds Ubuntu's ready-made packages, kept up to date with Software Update. **Build them on this computer** fetches FFmpeg's source and builds it with every format (H.264 and H.265 encoders, AAC and more) after the first start, in the background; it takes a while, and you see exactly what goes in. Some formats are covered by patents in some countries: you decide whether you may use them where you live.

## After the first start
What needs the internet (media formats, fonts, drivers, apps from Flathub) and your things from Windows come in the background, the first time Huginn is online, so installing isn't any slower. Nothing in your home is overwritten.

## Encryption and the recovery key
Choose **With a password**: it's asked for when the computer starts, before anything else. The installer also shows a **recovery key** of eight words: write them down or take a photo. They open Huginn if the password is forgotten; nobody, Huginn included, can open it otherwise.

## While it installs
About ten minutes. The steps tick off as it goes and the line fills; **Show Details** shows what it's doing, and you can keep trying Huginn meanwhile. When it's done, press **Restart Now** and take out the installation drive when the screen goes dark.

## If it stops
It says why. **Save the Log** keeps what happened in your home folder, and **Back to the Summary** lets you change something and try again.
