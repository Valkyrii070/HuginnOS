# Clock
World clock, alarms, stopwatch and timer.

## Times elsewhere
Add a City finds any place by name; point at a city and press × to take it off.

## Alarms
New Alarm: type a time (7:30, 19:30 or 7:30 pm), say what it's for if you like, and choose the days it repeats (none: it rings once and turns itself off). An alarm rings from the dock's live island with Stop and Snooze (nine minutes), whether or not Clock is open, while the computer is on and you're signed in. One missed by more than ten minutes (the computer was off or asleep) isn't rung late.

## Stopwatch and timer
Laps on the stopwatch. The timer takes one of the lengths shown or one you type (25, 1:30, 2h 15m, 45s); it shows in the live island and rings there when it's done. The timer runs while Clock is open.
