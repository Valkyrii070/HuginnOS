# Print & Scan
Printers and scanners.

## Print
Most printers on your network or plugged in by USB appear by themselves. Choose copies, both sides, pages, paper size, black only and sideways. "Print…" in Files prints straight from there.

## A printer that doesn't appear
Choose "Add a printer by its address" and type what the printer's own screen or network page says, like 192.168.1.40 or printer.local. Any printer made since about 2015 that prints over the network without a driver (AirPrint, IPP Everywhere, Mopria) works this way. Remove takes a printer off the list.

## Waiting to print
What hasn't printed yet is listed under the printers, with Cancel for each.

## Scan
Scan pages one after another, drop a bad one, then save them all as one PDF in Documents/Scans or send it by Mail.
