# Phone
Your phone and this computer, working together.

## Pair
Install the free KDE Connect app on your phone (Android or iPhone) and join the same Wi-Fi. Your computer shows up in the app: tap Pair there, or Pair in Phone. Both screens show the same code; if they match, accept.

## What you can do
Send files, words, a web address (it opens on the phone) and the clipboard, ring a lost phone and see its battery.

## From the phone
Its notifications appear here, with their buttons and a reply where the app allows one; clear one here and it clears on the phone. Calls show in the island, with Silence and a quick text back. Files it shares arrive in Downloads; links open; words are copied.

## If it doesn't show up
Both need to be on the same network. Open the KDE Connect app on the phone and pull down to refresh, or press Look again in Phone.
