# Help
These guides, on this computer.

## Find an answer
Type a few words in the big search field: Help looks through every guide.

## From an app
Every guide has a button to open its app.

## Something not right?
Settings › About › Make a Problem Report puts the logs, the hardware and any crashes in one .zip in Documents, to look over and send to whoever is helping you. Your name, your computer's name, network and Bluetooth names and addresses are left out, and nothing from your own files goes in. If the desktop won't start, sign in at a text console (Ctrl+Alt+F3) and type huginn-report.
