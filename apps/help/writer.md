# Writer
Letters, essays, reports and anything with formatting.

## Write and format
Type, then use the islands for styles, bold, italic, alignment, lists and tables.

## Comments
Select some words and press Ctrl+Alt+M (or right-click › Comment). Comments show in the Review tab on the left: click one to go to its words, Reply, Resolve when it's dealt with, or delete it.

## Track changes
Turn on Track changes in the Review tab (or Ctrl+Shift+R) before handing a document round. What anyone types is underlined in blue, and what they delete stays, struck through in red, signed with their name, until someone accepts or rejects it: one at a time with ✓ and ✗, from the right-click menu, or all at once. Word's own tracked changes and comments come in and go out with .docx files.

## Word and other suites' files
Writer opens and saves Word (.docx) and OpenDocument (.odt) files, so you can work with people who use other office apps.

## Export
Export makes a PDF or another format to send.
