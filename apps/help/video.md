# Media Player
The videos in your Videos folder, and any video you open or drop on it.

## Watch
Space plays and pauses; ← and → skip five seconds (with Shift, a minute); ↑ and ↓ or the wheel change the volume; M mutes; F fills the screen; 0 to 9 jump through the video; N and P go to the next and previous one.

## Speed, repeat and subtitles
The buttons at the top right (or [ and ], L and S): play from half speed to double, repeat the video, and show subtitles from a file beside the video with its name (film.srt, film.en.srt or film.vtt).

## Carry on
A long video you stopped partway through starts again where you left it.
