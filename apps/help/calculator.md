# Calculator
Exact sums with brackets, percentages and powers, and a scientific pad when you want one.

## Type
Use the keyboard or the keys. The answer shows as you type. Ctrl+C copies the answer and Ctrl+V pastes a sum.

## Scientific keys
The toolbar's first button (or View › Scientific keys, Ctrl+E) adds sin, cos, tan, ln, log, √, powers, x², factorials, π and e. Deg and Rad choose degrees or radians. You can type them too: sin(30), 2^10, √(9), 5!, 2π.

## Earlier sums
The clock button shows the sums you've finished (the last 50, kept between openings). Click one to carry on from its answer; Clear empties the list.
