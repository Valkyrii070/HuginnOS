# Terminal
Your command line.

## Tabs
Ctrl+Shift+T opens a tab and Ctrl+Shift+W closes it; Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown switch between them.

## Copy and paste
Drag over text to select it (double-click takes a word, a path or an address); Ctrl+Shift+C copies it, or everything when nothing is selected. Ctrl+Shift+V or the middle button pastes. The right button copies what's selected, or pastes when nothing is.

## Find and text size
Ctrl+Shift+F finds lines in what's been printed; Enter and the arrows go from one to the next. Ctrl and + or − change the text's size; Ctrl+0 puts it back.
