# Music
The songs in your Music folder, with their own titles, artists, albums and lengths.

## Play
Pick an album or a song (double-click, or Enter); search finds the rest. Space plays and pauses. What's playing carries on while you search or look at another album. Drop songs on the window to play them from anywhere.

## Shuffle and repeat
The two buttons beside the volume: shuffle plays the songs that were listed in a random order, each once; repeat goes round them all, or keeps to one song.
